# Quality Review

Review the generated file against these gates before delivery.

## Evidence gate

- Every employer, title, date, certificate, award, number, scale, ownership verb, and outcome traces to the source.
- Uncertain claims are removed, softened, or explicitly listed for confirmation.
- Collaborative work identifies the person's contribution without inflating ownership.
- Tools used only in tutorials are not presented as project mastery.

## Relevance gate

- The first half page makes the target role obvious.
- The most relevant evidence appears before weakly related material.
- Required skills from the JD are present only where the person has real evidence.
- Repeated or low-value content has been compressed.

## Language gate

- Every bullet begins with a meaningful action or result, not a vague label.
- Sentences are specific, concise, and free of empty adjectives.
- Terminology matches the target market while remaining understandable.
- Chinese and English variants are not literal word-for-word translations when idiom should change.

## Presentation gate

- Rendered page count matches the requested limit.
- No text is clipped, overlapping, orphaned, or hidden in an image.
- Section hierarchy is consistent and readable at normal zoom.
- Dates, contact details, links, and spacing are consistent.
- The document remains comprehensible when text is copied.

## Review report

Write a short `review.md` next to the resume:

```markdown
# Review — [Target role]

## Positioning
- Primary evidence used:
- Weak or omitted evidence:

## Needs confirmation
- 

## Integrity changes
- 

## Render result
- Markdown:
- HTML:
- PDF:
- Page count:

## Final verdict
- Ready / Needs user confirmation
```

Do not call a version “ready to submit” while an unresolved credential, employer, date, contact detail, or material claim remains.
